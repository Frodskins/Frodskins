## 🚀 **Quick Installation (3 Steps)**

### **Step 1: Download**
- Download one of the archives above, OR
- Copy the `StatForge-Release/StatForge/` folder directly

### **Step 2: Extract & Copy**
- **If using ZIP/TAR.GZ**: Extract the archive
- Copy the `StatForge` folder (not StatForge-Release) to your WoW AddOns directory:
  - **Windows**: `World of Warcraft\_classic_\Interface\AddOns\`
  - **Mac**: `Applications/World of Warcraft/_classic_/Interface/AddOns/`

### **Step 3: Verify Installation**
Your AddOns folder should now contain:
```
AddOns/
└── StatForge/
    ├── StatForge.toc
    ├── StatForge.lua
    ├── Core/
    │   ├── Constants.lua
    │   ├── Database.lua
    │   ├── GearScanner.lua
    │   ├── ReforgeEngine.lua
    │   └── StatCalculator.lua
    └── UI/
        └── StatForgeFrame.xml
```

## 🎮 **First Launch**

1. **Start WoW**: Launch Mists of Pandaria Classic
2. **Enable Addon**: At character select, click "AddOns" and ensure "StatForge" is checked
3. **Log In**: Enter the game with any character
4. **Open Addon**: Type `/statforge` or `/sf`
5. **Scan Gear**: Click "Scan Gear" to analyze your equipment
6. **Follow Recommendations**: Visit a Reforging NPC and apply suggestions

## 🛠️ **Troubleshooting**

### **Addon Not Appearing?**
- Verify folder name is exactly `StatForge` (case-sensitive)
- Check you're in the correct AddOns directory
- Try typing `/reload` in-game

### **No Recommendations?**
- Your gear might already be optimized!
- Ensure you have reforgeable stats (not primary stats)
- Try clicking "Refresh" in the addon window

### **Still Having Issues?**
- Check `INSTALLATION.txt` in the download package
- Review `README.md` for detailed documentation
- Ensure you're using Mists of Pandaria Classic (not retail WoW)
