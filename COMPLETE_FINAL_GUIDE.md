## 🎯 **HOW TO USE**

### **Step-by-Step:**

2. **View character**: See your portrait and character info
3. **Scan gear**: Click "Scan Gear" button
4. **Review recommendations**: Check the detailed list of suggested reforges
5. **Visit reforge NPC**: Apply the recommendations at an Ethereal Reforger

### **Understanding the Display:**
- **Character Panel**: Shows your portrait, name, class/spec, level
- **Current Stats**: Your actual hit, expertise, crit, haste, mastery values
- **Recommendations**: Stat priority and caps for your spec
- **Reforge List**: Specific items to reforge with exact stat changes
- **Summary**: Total reforges needed and estimated cost

## 🔍 **COMMANDS & DEBUGGING**

### **Main Commands:**
- `/sf` or `/statforge` - Open addon window
- `/sf scan` - Force gear scan and analysis
- `/sf debug` - Show detailed gear detection info
- `/sf hide` - Close addon window
- `/sf reset` - Reset all settings

### **If Something Goes Wrong:**
1. **Use debug mode**: `/sf debug` to see what's detected
2. **Try manual scan**: `/sf scan` to force analysis
3. **Refresh UI**: `/reload` to reset everything
4. **Check gear**: Make sure you have secondary stats on items
