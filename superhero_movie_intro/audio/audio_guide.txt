AUDIO GUIDE FOR SUPERHERO MOVIE INTRO
====================================

RECOMMENDED AUDIO FILES:
1. intro_music.wav - Main background music track
2. impact_sounds.wav - Sound effects for character entrances
3. whoosh_effects.wav - Movement and transition sounds

AUDIO SPECIFICATIONS:
- Format: WAV or AIFF (uncompressed) for best quality
- Sample Rate: 48kHz (industry standard)
- Bit Depth: 24-bit minimum
- Duration: 15-20 seconds (to match intro length)

MUSIC STYLE RECOMMENDATIONS:

EPIC ORCHESTRAL:
- Dramatic strings and brass
- Building intensity
- Heroic themes
- Examples: Hans Zimmer, John Williams style

ELECTRONIC/MODERN:
- Synth pads and arpeggios
- Electronic drums
- Futuristic sounds
- Examples: Tron Legacy, Marvel movie trailers

HYBRID ORCHESTRAL/ELECTRONIC:
- Best of both worlds
- Modern superhero movie feel
- Dynamic build-ups
- Perfect for character reveals

SOUND EFFECTS TIMING:
- 0-2s: Ambient intro, building tension
- 2s: Title impact sound
- 5s: Spider-Man entrance whoosh
- 7s: Venom emergence dark rumble
- 9s: Green Goblin flight/explosion sounds
- 11s: Final impact/hit
- 12s: "Coming Soon" reveal

AUDIO LEVELS:
- Music: -12dB to -6dB (leaves headroom)
- Sound Effects: -18dB to -12dB
- Final Mix: Peak at -3dB maximum

ROYALTY-FREE MUSIC SOURCES:
- Epidemic Sound
- AudioJungle
- Freesound.org (for SFX)
- YouTube Audio Library
- Adobe Stock Audio

MIXING TIPS:
- Use EQ to separate frequency ranges
- Add reverb for cinematic space
- Compress for consistent levels
- Side-chain music to SFX for clarity

Place your audio files in this folder and import them into After Effects to complete your superhero intro!
