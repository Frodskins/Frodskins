SUPERHERO IMAGE PLACEMENT GUIDE
================================

Place your superhero images in this folder with the following specifications:

REQUIRED IMAGES:
1. spiderman.png    - Spider-Man character image
2. venom.png        - Venom character image  
3. green_goblin.png - Green Goblin character image

IMAGE REQUIREMENTS:
- Format: PNG with transparent background (recommended)
- Resolution: Minimum 1500x2000px, 4K preferred for best quality
- Aspect Ratio: Portrait orientation works best (characters are typically taller than wide)
- File Size: Keep under 50MB each for optimal After Effects performance

CHARACTER POSITIONING TIPS:
- Spider-Man: Action pose, web-slinging or jumping works well
- Venom: Menacing pose, symbiote tendrils visible
- Green Goblin: Flying or hovering pose on glider

LIGHTING CONSIDERATIONS:
- Consistent lighting direction across all images
- Dramatic lighting from above/side creates cinematic look
- Avoid flat lighting - shadows add depth

COLOR MATCHING:
- Images should have similar contrast levels
- Avoid overly saturated colors that clash
- Consider the dark, cinematic background when selecting images

BACKGROUND REMOVAL:
- Clean transparent backgrounds are essential
- Use professional tools like Photoshop for best results
- Check edges for halos or leftover background pixels

Once images are placed here, run the After Effects scripts to automatically position and animate them in your composition.
