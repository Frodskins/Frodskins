======================================================
 StatForge - WoW MoP Classic Reforging Assistant
======================================================

SIMPLE INSTALLATION:

1. Copy the "StatForge" folder from this download
2. Paste it into your WoW AddOns directory:
   
   Windows: World of Warcraft\_classic_\Interface\AddOns\
   Mac: Applications/World of Warcraft/_classic_/Interface/AddOns/

3. Your final path should be:
   AddOns/StatForge/StatForge.toc

4. Launch WoW MoP Classic
5. Type: /statforge

That's it! The addon will scan your gear and show reforge recommendations.

Commands:
- /statforge or /sf = Open addon window
- /statforge scan = Manually scan gear

Enjoy optimized stats!
